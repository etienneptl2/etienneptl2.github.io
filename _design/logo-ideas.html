EP Labs: logo ideas

Six minimal directions. Each shows the mark large, in the header, at favicon sizes (32px and 16px) and on a dark background.

Accent:

1Monogram tile

Lowercase "ep" in a rounded square, the same shape as an app icon, so it sits naturally next to Budgie, Stockey and Tap.

2Geometric e + p

Both letters drawn from circles and straight strokes, the way the app icons are built. Works as a line drawing at any size.

3"ep." with an accent dot

The full stop is the only colour. It suggests a finished, shipped product.

4App grid

Four rounded squares, one filled: a studio of apps, with the latest highlighted. No letters, so it reads in any language.

5Flask

The literal "Labs" reading, as a single-weight outline with one bubble in the accent colour. Playful but still quiet.

6Wordmark only

No symbol: "ep" in a heavy weight, "labs" in a light one. The simplest option, and the favicon becomes just the "ep".

Option 6: where the coral goes

The bar in the first sketch was meant as a text cursor, "still building", but it doesn't read clearly. These are four clearer ways to place the accent.
